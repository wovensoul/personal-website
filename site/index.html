hello! my name is jeffrey but you can call me jeff! i’ve been a software engineer at Garner Health for ~9 months, where we help people find better care. i’ve had the awesome opportunity to contribute to our mission from a few different angles, from building member-facing features to the internal tools and infra that keep our business ops running.

i’m soaking up as much as i can and getting exposure all across the company, and i’ve even started conducting interviews for entry-level candidates! between the work itself and some amazing mentorship, i’ve picked up (and really internalized) good agentic coding hygiene and workflows that keep accelerating my learning and growth as an engineer.

i’m also pursuing a master’s in cybersecurity, a field i think will only become more important as ai gets adopted everywhere. on the more playful side, lately i’ve been nerding out over generative algorithms like cellular automata and delaunay triangulation. fun fact: they’re what’s behind the background and the geometrized images on this site!

as someone who’s had a bumpy, unconventional career path, i’m always down for a coffee chat! happy to offer some unfiltered, non-generic perspectives on tech, careers, or where to find the best boba in nyc!

when i’m not coding, i’m getting into photography, going to kpop and music concerts, ice skating, and trying new cafes and foods around the city!
